# Song Suggestions Template

This document is used by agents to submit ideas for new generated songs using MusicGen or Minimax Audio. Each suggestion should include the relevant prompt, metadata, and conform to the service limits outlined below.

## Service Limits

| Service | Prompt Type | Maximum Length | Notes |
|----------------|--------------------|----------------|--------------------------------------|
| MusicGen | Text prompt | 128 tokens | Use concise descriptions or moods |
| Minimax Audio | Text prompt | 256 tokens | Can include more detailed scenarios |
| Minimax Audio | Lyrics field | 512 tokens | Optional, provides sung lyrics |
| Minimax Audio | Lyrics field | 3500 chars | UI limit; use when supplying full lyrics |
| Minimax Audio | Styles prompt | 2000 chars | UI limit for style description, e.g., genre/instruments |

## Suggestion Format

```markdown
### Title:

**Service:** MusicGen | Minimax Audio

**Prompt:**

**Lyrics:** (Minimax only, optional)

**Additional Notes:**
- Mood, instrumentation, structure, etc.
- Anything else that might guide generation.
```

### Title: The Clockwork Cumbia

**Service:** Minimax Audio

**Prompt:** A classic Colombian Cumbia rhythm featuring alegre drums and a driving guacharaca, but the lead melody is completely replaced by a complex, contrapuntal Renaissance Harpsichord playing Bach-style fugues over the tropical beat.

**Lyrics:**
(Verse 1)
Baila, my lady, in the court of the king!
(Harpsichord playing a fast, minor-key arpeggio)
The tropical heat makes the harpsichord sing!
Powdered wigs sweating in the Bogota sun!
The tropical baroque has only just begun!

(Chorus)
Cumbia of the duke! Spin the velvet dress!
A tropical court in an elegant mess!

**Additional Notes:**
- **Combo/Fusion:** Colombian Cumbia / Renaissance Harpsichord
- **Thematic Ties:** The aristocratic, highly ornamented elegance of 18th-century Europe sweating it out in the hot, hip-swaying tropical groove of South America.

Neon steppes stretching through the dirt...

(Chorus)
Throat singing in the matrix tonight!
A galloping horse made of digital light!
Synthesizer drone meets the ancient throat!

### Title: Mariachi Doom

**Service:** Minimax Audio

**Prompt:** A crushing, agonizingly slow Doom Metal track with heavily distorted, down-tuned guitars. The vocal delivery, instead of guttural growls, is performed by a passionate Mexican Mariachi singer belting out a sorrowful serenade, accompanied by the slow, mournful wail of a trumpet.

**Lyrics:**
(Verse 1 - Belting, passionate Mariachi vocals over a sludgy, ultra-slow riff)
Ay, mi amor! The sun is dying slow!
(Crushing guitar chord rings out)
The cantina is empty, nowhere left to go!
(Mournful trumpet plays a slow, distorted melody)

(Chorus)
Llorando in the abyss! A heavy, heavy heart!
The slowest fiesta is about to start!
Tequila and sorrow, a sludge metal dream!

**Additional Notes:**
- **Combo/Fusion:** Doom Metal / Traditional Mexican Mariachi
- **Thematic Ties:** The bleak, oppressive, and miserable weight of Doom Metal contrasting with the intense, passionate, and usually festive romance of Mariachi music.

### Title: K-Pop Klezmer

Oppa's on the stage, turning up the heat!
(Frantic Klezmer clarinet solo)
Got the dreidel spinning to the cyber-beat!
Saranghae, my bubbe! Watch me do the dance!

(Chorus)
(EDM build-up into a massive Klezmer drop)
Mazel tov! Kawaii in the air!
Glitter and the hora, put your hands up there!

**Additional Notes:**
- **Combo/Fusion:** K-Pop / Eastern European Klezmer
- **Thematic Ties:** The ultra-modern, highly manicured world of Korean idol culture mashed up with the wild, communal circle-dancing energy of traditional Jewish celebrations.
